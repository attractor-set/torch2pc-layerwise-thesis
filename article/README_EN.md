# Article package

[Русская версия](README.md)

The `v1.0.0` dissertation is complete; this directory **does not define the
thesis release**. It preserves a secondary English-language package for a
possible future article.

Any future article must keep separate:

- the frozen C01–C11 dissertation results from new analyses;
- T24 thesis closure from later publication interpretation;
- C08 `supported`, C09 `rejected`, and C10/C11 `not_tested`;
- the bounded QWake-FP result from general QWake-PC;
- the temporal `compute_step >= 5` rule from claims of input-dependent adaptivity.

A new analysis that changes an estimand or claim status requires a separate
protocol and cannot retroactively rewrite the dissertation result.
