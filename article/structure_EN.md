# Derived-article status

[Русская версия](structure.md)

The `v1.0.0` dissertation is complete; this directory is **not the canonical
dissertation text**. Existing `manuscript_EN.tex` and `supplementary_EN.tex`
files are retained as historical article scaffolds and do not automatically
represent the final C01–C11 results.

If a separate article is prepared after `v1.0.0`, its structure must be rebuilt
from the closed claim registry with an explicit publication scope. At minimum:

1. formulate a narrow article question without expanding dissertation bounds;
2. bind each primary statement to C01–C11 and
   `thesis/data/thesis_traceability.json`;
3. preserve C08 `supported`, C09 `rejected`, and C10/C11 `not_tested` without
   reinterpretation;
4. do not present temporal rule `compute_step >= 5` as input-dependent
   adaptivity;
5. separate completed dissertation results from any new, independently
   registered follow-up experiment;
6. recheck bibliography, numbers, provenance, and external-validity limits
   before article publication.

The current `v1.0.0` release publishes the dissertation and its source
repository, not a completed journal manuscript.
