\section{Purpose of the Registry}

The repository glossary is the normative source of terminology for documentation,
protocols, reports, and the dissertation. Terminology therefore must not exist in two
disconnected variants, one in \code{docs/glossary_EN.md} and another in the main
text. This appendix contains the complete English registry of all stable
\code{TERM-*} identifiers present in the normative glossary at dissertation build
time.

Concepts needed directly for the research questions and result interpretation are
introduced in the main chapters. In particular, Chapter~\ref{ch:related-work}
defines PC-TREF, PC-CATM, and QWake-PC/QWake-FP before the experiments are
described. This appendix serves a different role: it is a verifiable registry in
which every normative term is presented with its canonical meaning and usage
boundary inside the dissertation itself.

Presence of a term in this registry does not imply empirical confirmation of the
corresponding mechanism. Sections concerning prospective extensions of theory and
control, and reserved generalizations of computational action, contain concepts
whose meanings are fixed for unambiguous usage but which lie outside the mandatory
empirical claims of the present work.

\section{Completeness and Source}

The registry below is generated automatically from \code{docs/glossary_EN.md}
during \code{make thesis}. The build checks equality of the ordered TERM-* identifier
set against \code{docs/glossary.md}, identifier uniqueness, presence of an English
definition and usage rule for every term, and complete inclusion in the dissertation.
The current repository state expects 117 of 117 terms.
